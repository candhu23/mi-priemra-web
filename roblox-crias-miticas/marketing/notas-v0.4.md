## EN
🐣 Mythic Hatchlings is open! Hatch eggs and collect 72 pets across 9 islands.
🌀 Fuse 5 alike at the Altar to rank them up: Common → … → Mythic.
🪨 Each Mythic you create gives a Rebirth Stone for your Upgrade Garden (12 upgrades).
📖 Discover pets in the Index to open new islands.
✨ 1% Shiny pets (x2 power) – 200 Shiny score opens the Aurora Sanctuary.
🌟 Golden Crystal every 3 min · 3 daily missions · 7-day rewards.
